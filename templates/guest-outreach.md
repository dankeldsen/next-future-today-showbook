# Guest Outreach Template - Next Future Today

Booking flow and email templates. All templates assume a short, specific, researched ask - the "I actually know your work" opener is the difference between a yes and silence.

---

## Booking flow

1. Identify guest + fill the guest-research doc (at least the basics + the angle) BEFORE outreach.
2. Send outreach email (template below). One follow-up after 5 business days. Then let it go.
3. On yes: confirm date/time, format (video + audio), collect bio + headshot, send guest promo kit after the episode is live.
4. Add to INDEX.md with status `booked`.

## Outreach email - cold

Subject: Next Future Today - [one-word episode concept] on [topic]

> [Name] - I host Next Future Today, a biweekly show on agentic AI meeting audit, risk, and compliance in regulated enterprises (33+ episodes; recent guests include a Forrester VP Principal Analyst on automation and the Acting CIO of the US International Trade Administration).
>
> Your [specific work - book/report/talk/post, named precisely] landed with me because [one honest sentence on why]. I'd love 45 minutes on the specific question of [episode thesis - not "your career"].
>
> Format: video + audio, biweekly release. You'll get the full promo kit (clips, quote cards, links) to use however you like.
>
> 20 minutes of your research time before we talk is all I ask - I'll come prepared.
>
> [Calendar link]

## Outreach email - warm intro / repeat guest

> [Name] - great seeing you at [event/context]. Following up on the [specific thing you discussed]: that's an episode. Want to do it properly on Next Future Today? Same format as [their prior episode / our usual]: 45 minutes, video + audio, [episode thesis]. I have [two dates] open.

## The guest promo kit (sent after episode is live)

- Video clip (60-90s) of their best moment
- 3 quote cards with timestamps
- Episode links (site, Apple, Spotify, YouTube)
- Pre-written social copy they can paste or adapt
- Episode-notes link

## The rules

- Never book a guest you can't research - the prep sheet depends on it.
- One follow-up, then stop. Silence is an answer.
- Guests promote when you make promotion effortless. The promo kit IS the marketing strategy.
